Cache-Control: public, max-age=60, stale-while-revalidate=30

This demonstrates modern caching with stale-while-revalidate (SWR).

  • max-age=60: Content is fresh for 60 seconds
  • stale-while-revalidate=30: After 60s, serve stale content while fetching fresh version in background

Use cases: News feeds, social media timelines, frequently updated content

How to observe SWR:

1. Make initial request: curl -I http://cache-proxy/news/

2. Check the Age header in subsequent requests

3. After 60+ seconds, observe background revalidation behavior
